Dinosaurs were a diverse group of reptiles that first appeared during the Triassic Period, more than 230 million years ago. They became the dominant land animals for millions of years and developed into many different shapes and sizes. Some dinosaurs walked on two legs, while others walked on four, and their diets included both plants and meat. Although most dinosaur groups became extinct about 66 million years ago, birds are considered the surviving descendants of certain dinosaurs.
